# Content run: Mirror bubbles and signs (creatures that mirror real life)

- GDD sections: §1., §2.3, §2.4, §2.5, §3.


## Draft 1

- writer: 8338 tokens out; validator: PASS
- critic: 2 blockers, 6 minor; "Partly: the lines are gentle and never shame or escalate, which fits the pressure ceiling, but most are generic pet-sim filler with none of the game's wry Matrix/TRON warmth, Juniper's set is Flora's set with the noun swapped, and two lines state facts the real state may not support."
  - BLOCKER :flora :hungry "My jar's looking empty": The bubble fires when feeding is due, not when stock is low. The sample estate starts with 15 worms, so Flora would claim an empty jar while the jar's own sign reads "Worms: 15". The bubble and the sign would contradict each other, and the bubble would state something the reported count doesn't say. [§1 pillar 1 (real, not simulated); §3 twin rule (a twin shows only what its counterpart's real state says)]
  - BLOCKER :modes :memorial :hal-note "Done — {name}'s on the porch now as a memorial..." (also the "in the sun" / "sunny spot" lines): The memorial note is a template for any creature, but it puts every memorial pet on the porch. A memorial Flora stays at the canal, so for her the line is false. The sun lines carry the same assumption, which is softer but still wrong for a creature that lives in water. [§2.5 (memorial mode for any creature; each mirror has its own :home); §3 twin rule]
  - minor :modes :memorial :hal-note (all five): All five lines are the same sentence reworded (moved to memorial / no needs / just a place to remember). None of them acknowledges the loss itself. "Nothing left to track, just memory" and "Nothing to track now" talk about a pet's death in task-tracker terms, which reads cold. "Done —" sounds like a butler closing a ticket rather than a companion. [§2.5 (your twin says so kindly and truthfully); §3.2 (twin as companion)]
  - minor :juniper :hungry / :fed / :low vs :flora: Juniper's lines are Flora's with the noun swapped: "Mm, worms"/"Mm, hay", "Thanks for the worms"/"Thanks for the hay", "Any worms going?"/"Any hay going?", and identical :low templates. A horse fed twice a day at 07:00 should sound different from an axolotl fed every two days. [§2.5 (each creature is its own mirror); usefulness]
  - minor :flora :fed "Much better" "Full now"; :juniper :fed "That's the stuff" "Satisfied now"; :biscuit :walked "Good walk"; :biscuit :porch "Resting up": These are generic lines that could come from any pet game. None of them has the wry tone or the jacked-in TRON flavor the game is going for. [§1 (tone, cast: you're Neo)]
  - minor :modes :virtual :idle "{name} naps in the sun" "{name} watches the yard" "{name}'s just here for company": Three problems. First, these are third-person narration in a speech bubble, while every other bubble speaks as the creature. Second, "watches the yard" and "naps in the sun" repeat Biscuit's :porch lines and don't fit a virtual water creature. Third, "just here for company" breaks the fiction by naming the mode. They do correctly carry no needs. [§2.5 (virtual mode for any creature; no needs)]
  - minor :modes :memorial :plaque "{name}, {years}" vs "{name} — {years}"; "{name} rested here, {years}": The first two lines are the same plaque apart from punctuation. "Rested here" is ambiguous: it can read as a burial claim, or as if the pet lived on this estate during those years. [§2.5 (memorial is kind and truthful)]
  - minor :flora :stock-sign / :juniper :stock-sign: Five synonyms for one label is low-value variety. The game will show one sign on the jar or hay pile, and rotating between near-identical labels adds no meaning. The counts themselves are correct and come from player-reported state. [§1 pillar 1; usefulness]

## Draft 2

- writer: 2229 tokens out; validator: PASS
- critic: 1 blockers, 7 minor; "Mostly yes: the bubbles are light and gentle, the hungry lines never guilt or escalate, and the signs track the reported counts. But many lines are interchangeable templates, and one memorial note has Hal claim an action the GDD reserves for the player."
  - BLOCKER :modes :memorial :hal-note, line 4 "I've moved {name} to memorial...": Hal says he moved the pet. The GDD says only the player can add a memorial pet or move a live pet to memorial. The twin reports what the player did; it doesn't do it. A kind note that is not truthful breaks the memorial rule. [§2.5 (memorial: only you add one, only you move it), §3.2 (your agent never changes settings without you)]
  - minor :modes :memorial :hal-note, line 2 "Done — {name}'s a memorial now. Nothing to track, just memory.": "Done" and "Nothing to track" sound like a butler confirming a task, which is wrong for a pet's death. All five notes are also one sentence reworded ("no needs... just a place to remember them"), so there is no real variety. None of them says the GDD's truthful point that the chores stop counting toward your own chores done. [§2.5 (twin says so kindly and truthfully; chores stop counting), §1 cast (twin is a companion)]
  - minor :modes :memorial :plaque, "{name} rested here, {years}": This reads like a grave marker, as if the pet lived or is buried on the estate. That is not true of a virtual place, and "rested" is ambiguous. [§2.5 (memorial lines kind and truthful), §1 pillar 1 (real, not simulated)]
  - minor :flora :low and :juniper :low, "Worms running low: {stock}", "Jar running low: {stock}", "Low: {stock} left" (and the bale versions): The :low rule fires at 2 or fewer, which includes 0. "Running low: 0" misreports an empty jar. The five lines are also the same label shuffled, so there is little variety. [§2.5 (:rules [:<= :stock 2]), §1 pillar 1 (the sign is the count you reported)]
  - minor :flora :stock-sign and :juniper :stock-sign (all five each): These are near-identical relabelings ("Worms: / Worm jar: / Jar count: / Worms in jar: / Worm stock:"). A hand-lettered sign could carry a little character without losing the count. [§2.4 (the canal: a worm jar), §1 pillar 3 (readable at a glance)]
  - minor :flora :hungry "Is it five already?": The due state persists past 17:00 and never escalates, so at 9 pm this line reads oddly. It is harmless but clock-specific. [§2.3 (pressure ceiling: one steady overdue state), §2.5 (Flora :at "17:00")]
  - minor :fed generic lines: "Much better", "Full now", "Satisfied now", "That's the stuff", "Good walk", "Resting up"; parallel templates "Mm, worms"/"Mm, hay", "Any worms going?"/"Any hay going?": These are interchangeable across any creature and give no sense of an axolotl, a horse, or a dog. Flora and Juniper are mirrored line for line. [§1 (cozy, warm, wry voice), §2.5 (personality shapes behavior)]
  - minor :modes :virtual :idle, all five: These are written as third-person narration ("{name} naps in the sun"), but bubbles are the creature's own voice. "Watches the yard" and "naps in the sun" also assume a land animal, which may not fit a virtual fish. The lines correctly carry no needs or chores. [§2.5 (:virtual: no needs, never jacks you out; any creature)]
