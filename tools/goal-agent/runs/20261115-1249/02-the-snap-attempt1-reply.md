**WHAT:** Flora's `:feed` chore is now tagged `:fx :snap` in `estate.edn`. When `pm.world/chore-done` counts a chore carrying that flag, it stamps `world` with `:snap {:mirror id :at at}` (plain data, no three.js). `pm.plan/snap-pose` is a pure function of `world`/`now` that turns that into a 0..1 progress over `snap-ms` (1.1s) — it returns nil once nothing is live, keeping the scene plan's own positions state-independent as before. `pm.scene/play-snap!` runs every frame from `update!`: while a pose is live for a mirror, it drops a small hidden worm mesh (added once at `mount!` time) from above onto the water and shoots that mirror's body group up and back down (a sine arc peaking mid-snap), then resets both once it's over. The bubble popping and the jar count dropping were already handled by the existing label-refresh path, so marking Flora fed now plays worm-drop + lunge/snap + bubble-pop + jar-1 together.

**HOW TO SEE IT:** Run the game with the kit loaded, click Flora (or otherwise mark her feed chore done) once it's due — a worm falls near her jar, her body lunges up and snaps back down over about a second, her "I'm hungry" bubble disappears, and the jar's sign ticks down by one worm.
