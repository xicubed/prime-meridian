WHAT: A pending blueprint's ghost in the scene can now be approved or rejected. Approving (`pm.blueprint/approve-blueprint`) flips its status to `:approved`, and `pm.scene` un-ghosts its mesh in place (solid, no more wireframe/transparency) and hides its "proposed" sign — the change is real. Rejecting (`reject-blueprint`) flips it to `:rejected` and `pm.scene` removes its mesh and sign from the scene outright — the ghost vanishes. Both are no-ops for an unknown id, and a blueprint's status only triggers the scene change once, the frame it flips.

HOW TO SEE IT: Run the estate with the kit; the sample blueprint "bench by the mailbox" (resources/estate.edn) appears as a translucent wireframe ghost with a sign reading "proposed: a bench by the mailbox." Left-click it to approve — it turns solid and the sign disappears. Reload and right-click it instead to reject — the bench and sign disappear entirely.
