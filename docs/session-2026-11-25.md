## 2. Decisions

- **Winning the day** is the tutorial, building something by talking to Hal (chat or voice), feeding Flora, and jacking out and back in (Michael). No loss: an unfinished day rolls over.
- **On the public build you talk to Hal; the crew don't answer.** Simpler than pestering the help. Talking to a member directly stays an option with your own agents (a local model, or to save the leader's tokens).
- **The CREW tab is the way around**; WASD, drag and the trackpad are second.
- **Every in-game line avoids copying the film**: "I can open the browser. Leaving your world is up to you."
- **The video follows PLAY.md's walkthrough**, so a viewer can play along afterwards.
