**The learned brain in the game** (M-15, round `foal-policy-v0`): `src/pm/creature_brain.cljs` runs the policy with onnxruntime-web 1.31.1 next to MuJoCo WASM, same 0.002 s physics and 10-step control schedule as training (physics waits for a late inference). `:brain` is species data in `horse.edn`; admin panel: age picker and "drop again"; `?age=<n>`, `?brain=twitch`. ## 2. Decisions

- Train on the GPU, play on the CPU: MuJoCo Warp is CUDA-only; one foal in the browser costs ~0.3 ms/frame.
- Infinite energy while learning to stand and walk; stamina (tire, rest, try again) comes back in a later run.
- Ten percent of practice starts are already standing.
