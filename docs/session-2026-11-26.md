## 2. Decisions

- **The video is for people who've never heard of an agent harness.** The trailer voice says no
  jargon; the reveal is "You think you're playing a game. You're running a crew."
- **Shoot on the real game, not the demo:** typing to Hal, calling him, the crew in Herdr.
- **Shoot scene by scene** and narrate last, once the picture is locked.
- **Real versus imagined creatures** carry the mirror idea: Flora's feed is filmed at her real
  feeding time (she isn't due until Friday at 4:40, so that scene waits); Juniper is sample.
- **Herdr and the game mirror each other.** Whatever you say in the game shows in Herdr; what
  matters in Herdr comes back to the game, summarised. The twin answers the crew's permissions
  first (Michael: "you're smarter than him").
- **"Here" is the player's call** (Michael: "I am picking the location because I know it can go
  there… if it sucks I will just remove it, move myself a little and try again").
