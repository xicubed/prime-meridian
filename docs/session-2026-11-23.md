## 2. Decisions

- **A real build can be removed**, but only the way it was built: a labelled button, a preview of the loss (the red ghost), then a bridge-recorded step. This replaces "a real build can't be dismissed". A bot-drafted removal ("take the bench out") can come later on top.
- **Every action on a build is a labelled button** on one card with three modes (trial, real, removing); clicking the ghost still makes the trial.
- **Signs that promise what nothing does come down.** The lobby keeps its spot for errands, not its sign.
- **Our props wear the kit's materials** where a palette colour maps to one; the phone booth's red stays painted.
- **Props can be Blender models, made from scripts** (`tools/blender/`), committed with the game when they're ours. The booth keeps its own see-through glass.
- **Night light is cheap:** one small light per prop at most; glowing signs carry none.
