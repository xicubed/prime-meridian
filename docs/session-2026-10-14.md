# Design session, Oct 14, 2026: everything is a twin

*Captured by Hal. Builds on [`session-2026-10-13.md`](session-2026-10-13.md); items marked **→ v0.3** go into the next GDD revision. Pepper (the business manager) is in PR #11.*

## 1. Every character is a twin → v0.3

The game doesn't invent characters. Each one is the in-world body of something real:

| In the game | Real counterpart | What the twin shows |
|---|---|---|
| **The Oracle** (your avatar) | Michael | where you are and what you've declared; agent-you stands in when you're away |
| **Hal** | the Hermes bot itself, not a character played by a model | his status bubble is his live session; his memory is the bot's memory; when the bot learns a skill, Hal gains an ability on the estate |
| **Flora** | the real axolotl | hunger and the worm count come from her state file; you feed her in life, and she snaps in the canal |
| **Pepper** | a business-manager agent (#11) | the company's real payments state and what needs your signature |
| **Workers / crew** | real coding sessions on the repo | the branch, the tests, the PR |

Talking to Hal in the game *is* talking to the bot, as one conversation across chat, SMS, and the estate. Both of you have twins: when you're away, agent-you and Hal keep working the estate together.

**Rule (lean):** *no character without a real counterpart.* In demo mode the counterpart is a pretend channel (§3.4 of the Oct 13 notes), never a fake that pretends to be real. A twin can only show what its counterpart's state actually says: no invented progress.

## 2. The bots we really need

What exists on the bot Mac today versus what the GDD names:

| GDD name | Real counterpart | Exists? |
|---|---|---|
| Hal (Producer / Operator) | Hermes, default profile | **yes** |
| Courier (morning brief) | scheduled job, weekday mornings, by SMS | **yes**, runs as Hal |
| Phone booth | scheduled job: inbound texts become tasks every 2 min | **yes**, runs as Hal |
| Keeper (Flora) | worm-count state file | **partly**: the state exists, no feeding chore yet |
| Clerk (mail triage) | none (only newsletter filters) | no |
| Pepper (business) | none yet; would be a separate Hermes profile with read-only payments access | no, needed by B-1 |
| Dev crew (Claude Code) | `.claude/agents/` files in the repo | no, planned for week 2 |

**Lean → v0.3: a character means a separate identity.** It gets its own face only if it has its own profile, credentials, and memory. Jobs that run *as Hal* are **Hal's errands**: at 8:15 Hal walks to the notice board and pins the brief; when a text arrives, he answers the phone booth. That makes the twins honest (one bot, one body) and cuts the cast to what's real:

- **v1 cast:** the Oracle, Hal, Flora, workers. **Pepper** joins when the business needs her (B-1).
- **Courier, Phone booth, Keeper, Clerk:** Hal's errands. Any of them becomes its own character only once it is split into its own agent.
