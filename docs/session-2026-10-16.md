  - **The Construct as Neo's first day.** Hal (Morpheus) loads programs from a rack of every skill and MCP connector; the first sparring match is the first real loop; "free your mind" dissolves into your estate.
  - **Pretend connections** restated as a technical strategy (feasibility, API limits, testing, security).
  - **Physics: motion is physical, not canned mocap.** Agents and creatures are Rapier active ragdolls with a balance controller (stumble, recover, fall, get up); Flora swims as a physical spine. Every character moves through one motion-controller interface from week 2; physical motion lands in week 7.
